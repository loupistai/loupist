# V1 acceptance

First-release target: macOS 13+ on Apple Silicon only. Windows is not supported in this release. Local-first, single user, supported provider API key or account sign-in. No Loupist account, no trades, no telemetry, no V1 MCP endpoint. Custom MCP connections are clients of external read-only services, not a Loupist server endpoint.

A release is ready when a real user can complete these journeys on a clean signed install. Observe the answer and the persisted result; a preview fixture or a model's claim to have saved something is not sufficient.

1. **Onboarding to a first answer.** Complete Welcome → Connect → Create a Loupist. Read and acknowledge the AI notice, then verify it survives restart. Connect with an AI account or choose Look around first; Back returns to the previous step. Create shows templates and Name, suggested assets for supported templates, and Focus for Custom; avatar and tone use defaults and can be changed later in Loupist settings. Read the background AI usage notice and create the local folder. Home shows a checklist to follow something, teach a principle and get a morning briefing, plus Import holdings (CSV). Each step completes independently; the checklist stays until all three are done or hidden. Without an account, Connect leads, Follow opens Following, and model-dependent steps wait. Connect, ask a question, receive a completed answer and open its cited evidence. Connection failures offer retry or cancellation; failed runs remain visible. Onboarding creates no schedule.
2. **Follow assets and import holdings.** Open Following from the sidebar, even when empty; add and remove assets, including a bare six-digit KRX code. What you follow is shared across Loupists; each Loupist chooses its Watch-for checks. Following alone starts no checks on a new install. Use Watch all, inspect missing Data connections, open an asset and its recent alerts, then Pause, Resume and Check now; Check now waits while paused. These checks use no AI account and run hourly while the app is open or in the menu bar. Preview a holdings CSV, inspect errors and commit a valid preview; configured Korea Investment holdings are another read-only import path. The followed list and latest holdings survive restart. The Map marks matched entities as followed or held and identifies unmatched entries. These are research context, not orders or target allocations.
3. **Teach a principle and see it applied.** Teach a principle in Chat with Private chat off, or save one from answer feedback. Open Lens from the sidebar or header and inspect its saved statement, reason and example where supplied. Ask a relevant question in a new Chat and verify concrete checks against the principle; expand Used your principle in the answer receipt to inspect what it received. Edit or archive it and verify the saved state; transcript text or a delivery receipt alone does not prove application.
4. **See the Loupist learn.** With Map building and method learning enabled and an available helper model, retain dated Sources that support a repeated pattern. Observe completed Map building, new entities or relationships, and the learning review outcome in Loupist settings. A review may create or refine a reusable method, defer, or fail; unrelated facts must not force a method. Inspect its rationale and evidence in Lens beside Chat. For a revised method, inspect dated **Then / Now** summaries and the reason for the change. A later relevant Chat must use it. A newly created method alone does not prove revision history or transfer.
5. **Scheduled briefings in Home.** Ask your Loupist in Home to turn on a morning briefing. In Schedule, open its task detail, Pause/Resume and Run now, then inspect the delivered briefing in Home and its dated changes, sources, principle checks and conflicting evidence. Custom scheduled chats also offer Delete under More, with confirmation; briefing and results tasks are paused instead. Change time or prompt in Home. Schedule's Watching row opens Following. Recurring failures retry at the next occurrence and pause after three consecutive failures; missing AI credentials pause immediately. Open the linked Chat and verify unread state and failed runs after restart. Scheduled chats and briefings use the AI account each run and need the app open or in the menu bar. The briefing grounds any view in dated evidence, risks and uncertainty, and never trades.
6. **Journal entries and later reviews.** Save your own judgment with reasoning and, optionally, a dated, observable check. Open Map, then Library: Sources · Journal · Notes. Inspect the entry in Journal and the Loupist's saved analysis separately in Notes. Later, review a due check against dated evidence and inspect its outcome alongside the original judgment and frozen inputs. An ordinary research request must not become an invented user judgment. Editing or reviewing an entry must not rewrite its historical evidence.
7. **Map replay and export.** Open Map from the sidebar or header, then search, filter, inspect and follow evidence to retained Sources. Replay growth from stored first-seen timestamps; pause, scrub, change speed and check reduced motion. Under More → Export, save a PNG and, where the webview supports recording, a replay video through the native save dialog. Inspect the name, date, counts and research-companion disclosure. Replay shows accumulated knowledge, not historical market prices. Unsupported recording and cancellation must leave the Map usable.
8. **Settings and custom Data connections.** Find AI accounts, Data connections, and General → About & privacy, including the AI notice and What leaves this Mac. Check General's menu-bar toggle: it starts off, turns on for the first briefing or Watching if no choice was made, and preserves an explicit choice. Under Data connections → Add your own connection (advanced), add a URL, configuration JSON or explicit command, inspect discovered tools and confirm. Grant eligible tools and exercise a read; non-read-only and financial-action tools remain unavailable. Changed definitions require renewed approval. Verify per-Loupist enablement and Memory-sharing refusal when disabled. No connection may turn research into trading.

The multi-session growth scenario in [evals/world/growth-scenario-v1](apps/loupist/evals/world/growth-scenario-v1/README.md) records Map counts, growth events, learning reviews, scheduled-run state, answers and usage. Missing changes must be reported as observed limitations, with scenario flaws distinguished from product failures. CI scores and history diffs do not replace visible application of taught and learned knowledge.

Release and security checks remain separate:

- Confirm bundled connection requirements in Settings → Data connections. Keyless services do not imply that paid vendors or connectors needing account details are preconfigured. Built-in Chat tools need no loading step; bundled Skills and references are readable but read-only.
- After a local calculation in a Chat, turn on Private chat from the composer’s + menu, verify its eye-off chip and explanation that the chat is saved and sent to the AI provider, publish a Markdown artifact, and open its Preview and Source without restarting or opening a different Chat. Files and charts remain research output; durable Memory remains in the Loupist folder.
- Restart and reopen a copied Loupist folder, including `.loupist/`, to verify retained originals, Chats and Memory. Preserve an unsupported older database rather than silently rewriting it. Removing a Loupist from the app must leave its folder available to reopen.
- React submits typed intent; Rust owns credentials, permissions, persistence, Memory transactions and worker lifetime. Native file tools stay inside the selected folder, scratch, attachments and bounded instruction paths, with read-only access to bundled Skills. `run_python` runs in the pinned WASI worker, not a host shell. Credentials must not enter exported artifacts, model-visible tool results or logs.
- Qualify the release's recorded signing mode and signed updates on the supported platform. Browser preview captures do not qualify the native webview, file dialogs, persistence or a clean install.

Loupist is an AI research companion that can share its view; it is not a licensed adviser and never executes trades. Investment-judgment answers state reasons, dated evidence, risks, uncertainty and what would change the view.
