# V1 acceptance

First-release target: macOS 13+ on Apple Silicon only. Windows is not supported in this release. Local-first, single user, supported provider API key or account sign-in. No Loupist account, no trades, no telemetry, no V1 MCP endpoint. Custom MCP connections are clients of external read-only services, not a Loupist server endpoint.

A release is ready when a real user can complete these journeys on a clean signed install. Observe the answer and the persisted result; a preview fixture or a model's claim to have saved something is not sufficient.

1. **Onboarding to a first answer.** Complete Welcome → Connect → Create a Loupist. On Welcome, read and acknowledge the first-run notice that the Loupist can share a view, is not a licensed adviser and never trades; verify that acknowledgment survives restart. Connect using a supported account sign-in or API key. Choose a preset, review the name, focus, avatar and tone, and create the Loupist in its local folder to reach Chat. Ask a research question, receive a completed answer, and open its cited evidence. Connection failures offer retry or cancellation; failed Chat runs remain visible with an error. Onboarding creates no schedule; following assets and turning on briefings happen later.
2. **Follow companies and import holdings.** Add and remove companies in the watchlist. Preview a holdings CSV, inspect validation errors, and explicitly commit a valid preview; configured Korea Investment holdings are another read-only import path. The latest holdings snapshot and watchlist survive restart. The Map marks matched entities as followed or held and identifies unmatched entries. These are factual research context, not orders or target allocations.
3. **Teach a principle and see it applied.** Teach a principle in Chat with Private chat off, or save one from answer feedback. Open Lens from the header’s Panel kind menu and inspect its saved statement, reason and example where supplied. Ask a relevant question in a new Chat and verify that the answer applies the principle as concrete checks. Edit or archive it and verify the saved state; a line in the transcript alone does not pass.
4. **See the Loupist learn.** With World processing and method learning enabled and an available helper model, retain dated Sources that support a repeated pattern. Observe completed World processing, new Map entities or relationships, and the learning review outcome in Loupist settings. A review may create or refine a reusable method, defer, or fail; unrelated facts must not force a method. Inspect a learned method's rationale and evidence in Lens beside Chat. For a method that has actually been revised, inspect dated **Then / Now** summaries and the reason for the change. A later relevant Chat must use the saved method. One newly created method alone does not prove revision history or transfer.
5. **Scheduled briefings in the Thread.** Ask the Bot to turn on the morning briefing and run one against followed assets and retained evidence. Inspect the result delivered to the Loupist's Thread and its run in Scheduled tasks from the Thread menu, its dated changes, sources, principle checks and any conflicting evidence. Open the linked Chat; unread state and failed runs must remain understandable after restart. Scheduled execution requires the app to be running, including supported resident operation; it is not a cloud service. The briefing grounds any investment view in dated evidence, risks and uncertainty, and never trades.
6. **Journal entries and later reviews.** Save the user's own judgment with reasoning and, optionally, a dated, observable check. Open World from Panel kind and inspect it in the Library's Notes section. Later, review a due check against dated evidence and inspect its outcome alongside the original judgment and frozen inputs. The Loupist must not invent a user judgment from an ordinary research request. Editing or reviewing an entry must not rewrite its historical evidence.
7. **Map replay and export.** Open World from Panel kind, then search, filter and inspect the Map, follow evidence to retained Sources, and replay growth from stored first-seen timestamps. Pause, scrub and change playback speed; observe reduced-motion behavior. Export a PNG and, where the webview supports recording, a replay video through the native save dialog. Inspect the exported name, date, counts and research-companion disclosure. Replay shows accumulated stored knowledge; it is not a historical market-price chart. Unsupported recording and cancellation must leave the Map usable.
8. **Custom read-only connections.** Add a connection from a URL, configuration JSON or an explicit command, inspect the discovered tools, and confirm the connection. Grant eligible tools through the approval UI and exercise a read; tool descriptions cannot grant authority. Non-read-only and financial-action tools must remain unavailable. A changed tool definition requires renewed approval. Verify per-Loupist enablement and the connection's Memory-sharing control, including refusal when disabled; no connection may turn research into trading.

The multi-session growth scenario in [evals/world/growth-scenario-v1](apps/loupist/evals/world/growth-scenario-v1/README.md) records Map counts, growth events, learning reviews, scheduled-run state, answers and usage. Missing changes must be reported as observed limitations, with scenario flaws distinguished from product failures. CI scores and history diffs do not replace visible application of taught and learned knowledge.

Release and security checks remain separate:

- Confirm bundled connection requirements in Settings. Keyless services do not imply that paid vendors or connectors needing account details are preconfigured. Inspect the available Chat tools separately from external account connections.
- After a local calculation in a Chat, turn on Private chat, publish a Markdown artifact, and open its Preview and Source without restarting or opening a different Chat. Files and charts remain research output; durable Memory remains in the Loupist folder.
- Restart and reopen a copied Loupist folder, including `.loupist/`, to verify retained originals, Chats and Memory. Preserve an unsupported older database rather than silently rewriting it. Removing a Loupist from the app must leave its folder available to reopen.
- React submits typed intent; Rust owns credentials, permissions, persistence, Memory transactions and worker lifetime. Native file tools stay inside the selected folder, scratch and attachments. `run_python` runs in the pinned WASI worker, not a host shell. Credentials must not enter exported artifacts, model-visible tool results or logs.
- Qualify signing, notarization and signed updates on the supported platform. Browser preview captures do not qualify the native webview, file dialogs, persistence or a clean install.

Loupist is an AI research companion that can share its view; it is not a licensed adviser and never executes trades. Investment-judgment answers state reasons, dated evidence, risks, uncertainty and what would change the view.
