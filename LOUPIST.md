# V1 acceptance

First-release target: macOS 13+ on Apple Silicon only. Windows is not supported in this release. Local-first, single user, supported provider API key or account sign-in. No Loupist account, no trades, no telemetry, no V1 MCP endpoint. Custom MCP connections are clients of external read-only services, not a Loupist server endpoint.

A release is ready when a real user can complete these journeys on a clean signed install. Observe the answer and the persisted result; a preview fixture or a model's claim to have saved something is not sufficient.

1. **Onboarding to a first answer.** Acknowledge the AI notice on Welcome. Connect an AI account or choose Look around first. Choose a template and any suggested assets, or enter a focus for Custom. Continue to the name step, try Back, then continue again. Choose a name and face colour, read the background AI usage notice and create the Loupist. Verify the name and face in Bot’s header. Choose **Get a morning briefing** in the greeting and check the request in the composer. Clear the draft and verify that Schedule is still empty. Return to Bot and connect if you skipped it. Ask Bot to open a research session comparing two companies’ latest filings. Open the session from the sidebar to follow the work. When it finishes, restart. Verify that the notice stays acknowledged, return to Bot and open the result. Follow a citation in the saved answer.
2. **Follow assets.** In Following, submit a listing search and pick a result. Follow a second asset by name using the option to follow it as typed. Open the first asset’s page. If a price is saved, compare it with its source and stated date, use **Check now** to refresh it, and inspect the chart if the read supplies history. If a setup run finishes without a price, read its report in Bot. Without an AI account, choose **Check now** on the asset page and read why setup needs an account. Open Bot and verify it offers to connect one. Create another Loupist without starter assets and verify that Following is empty there. Switch back, stop following the second asset and restart. Verify the first asset is still listed with its saved research and the second is absent.
3. **Teach a principle and see it applied.** Teach a principle in Chat with Private chat off, or save one from answer feedback. Open Lens from the sidebar and inspect its saved statement, reason and example where supplied. Ask a relevant question in a new research session and point to the check in the answer that applies your principle. Expand **Read your principle** under the answer and check that it shows the rule the answer received. In Lens, check the principle’s read count and find the session under **Read in**. Edit or archive the principle, then reopen Lens and verify the saved change.
4. **See the Loupist learn.** Enable World building and method learning with a connected model. In two separate research sessions with Private chat off, investigate the same pattern at different companies using a different dated filing for each. Paste one filing excerpt directly into its session and ask to keep it as a Source. Open World → Library → Sources and check the passage, paste date and **User-provided** label. Ask each session to record its findings on a Lens topic about that company, citing its own Source. Inspect the new names or relationships in World. Open Lens → History and check the dated review outcome. A deferred review shows **Nothing worth keeping**. If the review deferred or failed, reopen History and verify the recorded outcome. If it created or refined a method, open the method and inspect its evidence and rationale. For a revision, check the dated **Then / Now** summaries. Ask about a new case in a later session, then reopen its answer and identify where it applied the method.
5. **Scheduled briefings in Bot.** Ask Bot to turn on a weekday morning briefing. Open its task in Schedule, pause it, resume it and choose **Run now**. When the briefing arrives in Bot, open its run and check the dated evidence behind what changed. Ask Bot to change the time and verify the next scheduled run. Custom tasks also offer Delete under More, with confirmation. Recurring failures pause a task after three consecutive failures. A rejected account or missing model pauses it immediately. A conditional scheduled check can stay quiet. When evidence crosses a line set on an asset page, Bot separately posts the observed value and a **Review thesis** action. Scheduled runs use the AI account and require the app to be open or in the menu bar. Restart, check the saved task time and run history in Schedule, then reopen the briefing from Bot.
6. **Journal entries and later reviews.** Save your own judgment with reasoning and a dated, observable check. Open World → Library → Journal and inspect the entry. Keep the Loupist’s own analysis as a research note and find it on the separate Notes tab. When the check is due, ask for a review against evidence dated on or after the check date. Reopen the entry and compare the recorded outcome with your original judgment and the evidence saved with it.
7. **World replay and export.** Open World from the sidebar, find a name, filter World and follow a claim to its retained Source. Under More, choose Replay. Pause, scrub and change speed to inspect when names and links first appeared. Check replay with reduced motion enabled. Export a PNG from More and, where recording is supported, export a replay video through the native save dialog. Cancel one export and check that World stays usable. Open the saved file and inspect the Loupist name, date, counts and AI disclosure.
8. **Settings and custom Data connections.** Find AI accounts and Data connections in Settings. Under General → About & privacy, open the AI notice and read **What leaves this Mac**. On a fresh profile the menu-bar setting starts off. Enabling the first briefing or custom task turns it on unless you chose otherwise. In the installed app, turn on **Open Loupist at login**, log out and back in, and open the hidden window from the menu bar. Under Data connections → Add your own connection (advanced), add a read-only service by URL, configuration JSON or a pinned local command. Review the discovered tools, allow an eligible read and use it in Chat. Verify that financial actions stay unavailable and a changed tool needs approval again. With **Save results to Memory** off, ask to save a result and check the refusal. Restart and inspect the connection’s saved access for each Loupist.

The multi-session growth scenario in [evals/world/growth-scenario-v1](apps/loupist/evals/world/growth-scenario-v1/README.md) records World counts, growth events, learning reviews, scheduled-run state, answers and usage. Missing changes must be reported as observed limitations, with scenario flaws distinguished from product failures. CI scores and history diffs do not replace visible application of taught and learned knowledge.

Release and security checks remain separate:

- Confirm bundled connection requirements in Settings → Data connections. Keyless services do not imply that paid vendors or connectors needing account details are preconfigured. Ordinary research tools load automatically. Loupist loads chart and scheduling tools on demand. Bundled Skills and their references are read-only.
- After a local calculation in a Chat, turn on Private chat from the composer’s + menu, verify its eye-off chip and explanation that the chat is saved and sent to the AI provider, publish a Markdown artifact, and open its Preview and Source without restarting or opening a different Chat. Files and charts remain research output; durable Memory remains in the Loupist folder.
- Quit before copying the whole Loupist folder, including `.loupist/`. Import the copy in a fresh app profile and verify retained originals, Chats and Memory. Following starts empty in that profile. Preserve an unsupported older database rather than silently rewriting it. Removing a Loupist from the app must leave its folder available to reopen.
- React submits typed intent; Rust owns credentials, permissions, persistence, Memory transactions and worker lifetime. Native file tools stay inside the selected folder, scratch, attachments and bounded instruction paths, with read-only access to bundled Skills. `run_python` runs in the pinned WASI worker, not a host shell. Credentials must not enter exported artifacts, model-visible tool results or logs.
- On macOS Apple Silicon, open the quarantined Developer ID download and verify its stapled notarization ticket. For the transition from 0.1.2, qualify a signed update with a saved Data connection key, then confirm that choosing Always Allow carries access into the next update from the same team. Browser preview captures do not qualify the native webview, file dialogs, persistence or a clean install.

Loupist is an AI research companion that can share its view; it is not a licensed adviser and never executes trades. Investment-judgment answers state reasons, dated evidence, risks, uncertainty and what would change the view.
