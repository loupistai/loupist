# Thesis review

Adapted from Anthropic financial-services, thesis-tracker (Apache-2.0).
See [upstream provenance](../../NOTICE.md).

Use a dated evidence table when updating an investment thesis:

| Pillar | Prior expectation | New observation and date | Supports / weakens / unresolved | What would resolve it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Recover the prior thesis from exact-read Memory or a user-supplied original.
Do not manufacture an earlier view when there is none. Identify the business
mechanism, supporting evidence, and disconfirming observation for each pillar.
Keep the strongest countercase alongside the preferred explanation.

Separate an event's occurrence, public announcement, and expected impact.
A catalyst calendar contains sourced or explicitly hypothetical dates; a
predicted event is not an observed result. Track delays and cancelled catalysts.

Explain what changed in the thesis and what remains untested. Improving results
do not automatically justify a higher valuation; distinguish operating progress
from how much the market price already implies. Use the Journal workflow to
record only the user's explicitly stated judgment. Preserve reusable analytical
lessons through Lens under the common Memory rules. Research alone does not
require a position action, an automatic stop loss, or a recurring monitoring job.
