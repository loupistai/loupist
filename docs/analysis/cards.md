# Cards outside the reply

These items are not the answer body. The reply timeline, numbers and readers are in [Where results appear](surfaces.md#where-each-item-appears).

| Item | Surface |
| --- | --- |
| What changed for a followed asset | Rows Rust built for its [asset page](../knowledge/what-changed.md#what-changed), each with a date and a citation chip (the original link when there is one, otherwise the Source, the Map or the page): the page's What changed and At a glance; up to three assets with their most material row in the morning message; up to two rows per asset in the briefing reader's What you follow; the read and its latest move at the top of the Map inspector's Facts for a matched entity; a crossed line as one home Thread message |
| Tables comparing assets, and where sources disagree | A card inside the answer, reserved with "Building…" while `grid_publish` runs: rows of assets and columns of measures (with period and basis), each value a [checked](grids.md#grid-cells) number chip, each empty cell its reason, sortable columns, a caption counting linked cells and cells to check, Copy (tab-separated), Export (CSV with sources or Markdown table, through `export_save`) and Ask; an as-of table adds "Known as of {date}". A conflict card lists each source with what it says, its date and basis and the one the answer used (that word stays on one line), then why they differ, with See on the Map when the entity has a disputed claim. Tables also list in the panel's Work tab but never open the panel by themselves |
| Discount-rate × growth grids (`dcf_grid`, `reverse_dcf`) | A [sensitivity card](calculation.md#sensitivity-cards) in place of an answer paragraph that is only a link to the grid's result, and behind the results card's Open the grid |
| Results before and after | The [results card](results.md#results-before-and-after) on the asset page: Expected, Reported and Difference per measure with a source under every value, then price implied, price reaction, call tone and Filed so far; in an answer, a paragraph that is only a link to a snapshot's `citation_url` becomes the compact card (table and Filed so far); the home Thread message and the briefing's What you follow show two measures |
| Watcher alerts | One dated, sourced sentence each: in the morning message, a home Thread message, the briefing's What you follow and Schedule › Watching (with each asset's last alert); the observations behind them are What changed rows on the asset page ([watchers](../knowledge/watchers.md#local-watchers)) |
