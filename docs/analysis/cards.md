# Cards outside the reply

These items are not the answer body. The reply timeline, numbers and readers are in [Where results appear](surfaces.md#where-each-item-appears).

| Item | Surface |
| --- | --- |
| A followed asset's thesis and crossed line | The read, its moves and lines on its [asset page](../knowledge/asset-pages.md); a crossed line as one Bot message with Review thesis and Undo |
| Tables comparing assets, and where sources disagree | A card inside the answer, reserved on still ruled paper with "Preparing" while `grid_publish` runs, where the step started: rows of assets and columns of measures (with period and basis, said once under the title when every measure shares them), each value a [checked](grids.md#grid-cells) number chip, each empty cell its reason (with the run's note, when it left one, on hover, focus or a click), sortable columns (a table with more measures than assets whose asset rows do not fit the card turns: measures as rows, assets as columns with their symbol and page link, the same cells, no sorting; the turn reads the width the asset rows need, so a resize cannot flip it back and forth), a caption counting the values with sources and the numbers to check, Copy (tab-separated), Export (CSV with sources or Markdown table, through `export_save`; both, like Copy, keep asset rows in either layout) and Ask; an as-of table adds "Known as of {date}". A conflict card lists each source with what it says, its date and basis and the one the answer used (that word stays on one line), then why they differ, with See on the Map when the entity has a disputed claim. Tables also list in the panel's Work tab but never open the panel by themselves |
| Discount-rate × growth grids (`dcf_grid`, `reverse_dcf`) | A grid result can be cited in an answer: a paragraph that is only its link stays that link |
